1ST DAY 
These are the glimpse of opening ceremony of 4th  LU International MUN. It was hosted at the elegant resident of Dr. Syed Ragib Ali where we warmly welcomed  532 delegates, 50 Executive Board members from various institutions.  Following the Opening Plenary Sessions, we concluded it with a captivating concert performance by 
“Bay of Bengal,” accompanied by several talented local bands and artists.



2ND DAY
On the second day, we convened all our activities within the premises of Leading University. Here, delegates participated in multiple committee sessions, engaging in negotiations, meaningful dialogue, and the drafting of formal documents that reflected the outcomes of their discussions.


3RD DAY
On the final day of our conference, the activities were conducted across two venues Leading University and the Kushiara International Convention Hall. Following the conclusion of the committee sessions, participants proceeded to the convention hall for Award Giving And Closing Sessions. We were graced  by the presence of Dr. Syed Ragib Al, Chairman of Leading University Trustee Board,  along with different distinguished officials, honourable Deans and Professors of various faculties, former Presidents and alumni, as well as parents of participating students.
